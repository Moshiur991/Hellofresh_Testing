# SmileCare Dental — Pricing Guide

> Prices are estimates in Canadian dollars and follow the current provincial dental fee guide. Your final cost depends on your specific needs and insurance coverage. A precise quote is provided after an exam.

## Common services (estimated)
- New patient exam: $95–$130
- Recall exam (existing patient): $55–$75
- Standard cleaning (per unit): $65–$90
- Dental X-rays: $30–$95 depending on type
- Basic filling: $150–$300
- Teeth whitening (in-office): $350–$550
- Invisalign: from $3,500 (full treatment; consultation required for exact quote)
- Crown: $1,100–$1,600
- Root canal: $600–$1,200 depending on tooth

## Payment plans
For treatments over $1,000 we offer interest-free payment plans over 3–6 months. Ask our team for details.

## Consultations
Invisalign and implant consultations are complimentary for new patients.
