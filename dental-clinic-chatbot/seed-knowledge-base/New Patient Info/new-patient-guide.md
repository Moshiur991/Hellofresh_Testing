# SmileCare Dental — New Patient Guide

## Welcome
We're glad you're considering SmileCare Dental. We welcome new patients of all ages.

## Your first visit
Your first appointment typically includes a comprehensive exam, any necessary X-rays, and a discussion of your dental goals. A cleaning may be done the same day or booked as a follow-up depending on your needs.

## What to bring
- A piece of photo ID
- Your dental insurance information (if you have coverage)
- A list of any medications you take
- Details of any relevant medical history (please share only a brief summary through the chat; detailed history is collected securely at the clinic)

## Arriving early
Please arrive 15 minutes before your first appointment to complete intake forms, or ask us to send them in advance so you can fill them out at home.

## Do you accept new patients right now?
Yes — we are currently accepting new patients, including children and families.

## Nervous about the dentist?
Let us know. Our team is experienced with anxious patients and will take time to make you comfortable.
