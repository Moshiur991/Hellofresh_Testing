# SmileCare Dental — Cancellation & Scheduling Policy

## Cancellation notice
We ask for at least **48 hours notice** to cancel or reschedule an appointment. This allows us to offer the time to another patient.

## Late cancellation / no-show fee
- Cancellations with less than 48 hours notice may be subject to a **$50 late cancellation fee**.
- Missed appointments with no notice ("no-shows") may be subject to a **$75 fee**.
- Fees are waived for genuine emergencies at the clinic's discretion.

## How to cancel or reschedule
You can request a cancellation or reschedule through this chat, by phone, or by email. Please note: a request submitted here is **not** a confirmed cancellation — our team will confirm it with you.

## Late arrivals
If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to reschedule your appointment to avoid delaying other patients.

## Deposits
Some longer procedures (e.g. implants, full orthodontic treatment) may require a deposit to hold the appointment. Our team will explain this when booking.
