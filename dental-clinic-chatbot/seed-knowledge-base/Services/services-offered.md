# SmileCare Dental — Services Offered

## Preventive & General Dentistry
- Routine cleanings and check-ups
- Dental exams and X-rays
- Fluoride treatments and sealants
- Gum disease (periodontal) care
- Fillings

## Cosmetic Dentistry
- Teeth whitening
- Veneers
- Bonding

## Orthodontics
- **Invisalign** clear aligners (we are a certified Invisalign provider)
- Traditional braces referrals

## Restorative Dentistry
- Crowns and bridges
- Dentures
- Dental implants (consultation and placement)
- Root canal therapy

## Oral Surgery
- Simple and surgical tooth extractions
- Wisdom tooth removal

## Emergency Dentistry
- Same-day emergency appointments when available (see Emergency Info)

## What to do before your appointment
For a standard cleaning or check-up, no special preparation is needed. For surgical procedures, our team will provide specific instructions when your appointment is confirmed. Please bring a list of any medications you take and your insurance details.
