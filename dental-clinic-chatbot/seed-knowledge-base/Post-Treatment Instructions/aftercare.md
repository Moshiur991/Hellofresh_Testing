# SmileCare Dental — Post-Treatment Instructions

> General aftercare guidance only. Always follow the specific instructions given by your dentist. If you experience severe pain, heavy bleeding, or signs of infection, contact us or seek urgent care.

## After a filling
- Numbness may last a few hours — avoid chewing until it wears off to prevent biting your cheek or tongue.
- Mild sensitivity to hot/cold for a few days is normal.

## After a tooth extraction
- Bite gently on gauze for 30–45 minutes to control bleeding.
- Do not rinse vigorously, spit, or use a straw for 24 hours (this can dislodge the clot).
- Avoid smoking for at least 48 hours.
- Stick to soft foods for the first day; avoid the extraction site when chewing.
- Some swelling is normal; a cold compress can help for the first 24 hours.

## After a root canal
- Avoid chewing on the treated tooth until any permanent restoration is placed.
- Mild tenderness for a few days is normal and usually eases with over-the-counter pain relief.

## After teeth whitening
- Avoid staining foods and drinks (coffee, red wine, tea, berries) for 48 hours.
- Temporary sensitivity is common and typically resolves within a day or two.

## When to call us
Contact SmileCare Dental if you have pain that worsens after 2–3 days, swelling that increases, a fever, or bleeding that does not stop.
