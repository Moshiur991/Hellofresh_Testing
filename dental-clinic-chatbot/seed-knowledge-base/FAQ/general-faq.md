# SmileCare Dental — General FAQ

## Hours
- Monday–Wednesday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- Thursday: 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM
- Friday: 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
- Saturday & Sunday: Closed

## Location
SmileCare Dental is located at 123 Example Street, Suite 200, Toronto, ON M5V 1A1. We are on the second floor, accessible by elevator.

## Parking
Free patient parking is available in the lot behind the building. Street parking (metered) is also available on Example Street. The nearest public transit stop is Example Station, a 3-minute walk away.

## Are you accepting new patients?
Yes. SmileCare Dental is currently welcoming new patients of all ages, including children. New patients should arrive 15 minutes early to complete intake forms, or fill them out in advance (see the New Patient Guide).

## How do I book an appointment?
You can request an appointment through this chat, by phone, or by email. Please note: requests made through the chat are not confirmed bookings — our team will contact you to confirm availability.

## Do you see children?
Yes, we are a family practice and welcome patients of all ages.

## What languages do you speak?
Our team provides service in English and French.
