# SmileCare Dental — Insurance & Billing

## Do you accept insurance?
Yes. We accept most major dental insurance plans and are happy to submit claims on your behalf.

## Plans we work with
We accept plans from most major Canadian providers, including Sun Life, Manulife, Canada Life, Green Shield, and others. We are a participating provider for the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) — please confirm your eligibility with us.

## Direct billing
We offer **direct billing** to most insurance companies, so you only pay any remaining balance not covered by your plan at the time of your visit.

## What if I don't have insurance?
Uninsured patients are always welcome. We offer transparent pricing (see the Pricing Guide) and can discuss payment plan options for larger treatments.

## Payment methods
We accept cash, debit, Visa, Mastercard, and American Express.

## Questions about coverage
Coverage varies by plan. We recommend confirming your specific benefits with your insurance provider. Our front-desk team can help you understand your estimate before treatment.
